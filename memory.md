## 2. Investment Methods Playbook

**7-Step Supply Chain Mapping (verbatim from posts, consistent 2025-2026):**

1. **Identify the Physical Constraint** — where AI scaling (trillions CapEx, NVDA 800VDC shift, copper→photonics) hits a wall (bandwidth, power, yield, materials, precision motion for Physical AI).
2. **Full Vertical Map Bottom-Up** — raw (InP/SOI/TFLN/epi) → substrate → epi/processing → lasers/components (sole-source signals) → modules (pluggable/CPO/LRO/OCS) → systems (Google OCS, NVDA CPO) → end customers. BOM analysis.
3. **Hunt Primary Signals Over Narratives** — design-in/qualification, sole/primary supplier status, ramp hints from fireside/ER (ignore stale 2024 revenue), M&A, CHIPS Act, index inclusions.
4. **Valuation Asymmetry** — public MC vs private raises (Ayar/Lightmatter $4-15B+), TAM/pricing power, historical bottlenecks (Neon 1000-2000%, Dysprosium 2300%). "If a $700M company is the single point of failure... what value?"
5. **Ownership Shift Validation** — retail → institutions (or cross-border) as frontrunning + liquidity catalyst (SIVE US/West 58.7%, Blackrock/Vanguard inflows).
6. **Catalyst & Timeline Mapping** — mass-prod windows (3-10mo), listings (NASDAQ for SIVE), index adds (MSCI/Nasdaq), CapEx, geo/export controls (China Ga/Ge/In). Include NEGATIVE catalysts (design-out, in-package integration).
7. **Creative Overlaps & Risk Layering** — same tech across verticals (SIVE in DC + Aeva robotics). Explicit risk callouts (geo AXTI, dilution AAOI/NBIS, design-out some TW, foundation RPI). "I never typically say '1' because they all have different risk-profiles." Layer within the secular wave.

**Risk/Sizing Rules:**
- Call risks every time (geo/export, dilution, design-out, execution, structural).
- "Not a major position" for higher-risk/opportunistic (RPI).
- Core chokepoints (SIVE, AXTI) = heavy conviction / "not selling a single share".
- Options for pure vol (EWY) or leverage/safety in binary risk (AXTI calls).
- % thesis validation > dollar P&L. "I don't post dollar amounts because they don't matter."

**Add / Hold / Trim:**
- **Add** on strengthening primary signals (new sole-source confirmation, ownership filings, ramp timeline from fireside).
- **Hold** through noise if thesis intact ("not selling" despite media or influencer bear posts).
- **Trim** only on clear thesis invalidation (design-out confirmed, ramp failure, major geo block).

**Risk-Profile Archetypes (for sizing new names):**
- **SIVE-profile** = sole-source chokepoint, multi-vertical, deep moat → core heavy, "once-a-generation".
- **AXTI-profile** = material chokepoint w/ binary geo risk → high-beta, use options for safety.
- **AAOI-profile** = strong business / real volume but known structural drag (dilution, customer concentration, contested layer) → medium position, not core.
- **RPI-profile** = opportunistic, structural risk (foundation ownership) → "not a major position".
